How to see what you hear (Zoom, a coach, a clip)

Your browser can only listen to inputs. To track the sound your computer is playing, route it into a virtual input:

Mac: install the free BlackHole 2ch driver. In Audio MIDI Setup create a Multi-Output Device that ticks both your headphones and BlackHole, and pick it as the Mac’s output (Control Center ▸ Sound). Then choose BlackHole 2ch under “what I hear” here. Zoom’s speaker should be “Same as System”.

Windows: install VB-Cable, set the system output to CABLE Input, listen through Windows “Listen to this device” on your headphones, and choose CABLE Output here.

Anything inside Chrome (a YouTube clip, Zoom in the browser): choose “a browser tab”, then pick that tab and tick “Share tab audio”.

Colours are the note itself: violet E2 → blue A2 → cyan D3 → green G3 → olive A#3 → orange C#4 → pink E4 → red G#4. The top is deliberately loud so a jump is obvious even after your voice has come back down.

Keep it over Zoom: in Chrome, ⧉ float (top right) opens the two live notes in a small window that stays on top of every other app; drag its edges to resize.
